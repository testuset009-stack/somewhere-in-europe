#1 [IS]
This North Atlantic volcanic island is famous for spouting geysers, jet-black basalt beaches, and Europe's largest ice cap, Vatnajökull.
+++
Green ribbons of the northern lights frequently shimmer across its winter skies.
---
Iceland — Reykjavik. Dettifoss here is one of the most powerful waterfalls in all of Europe.

#2 [NO]
Deep glacial sea inlets called fjords carve past sheer kilometer-high cliffs in this Nordic nation, with waterfalls plunging directly into saltwater.
+++
The famous Trolltunga cliff juts horizontally 700 meters above a mountain lake.
---
Norway — Oslo. Sognefjord reaches more than 200 kilometers inland from the coast.

#3 [CH]
The sharp pyramidal peak of the Matterhorn and Europe's highest glacier railway station, Jungfraujoch, rise in the Alps of this nation.
---
Switzerland — Bern. The Great Aletsch Glacier here is the longest river of ice in the Alps.

#4 [UA]
Rolling Carpathian ridges crowned by Mount Hoverla and tranquil alpine Lake Synevyr grace the southwest of this country.
+++
Elsewhere, ancient beech forests and the unique Askania-Nova steppe reserve shelter rare wildlife.
---
Ukraine — Kyiv. Primeval Carpathian beech forests here belong to UNESCO's natural World Heritage sites.

#5 [IT]
This southern nation hosts mainland Europe's only active volcano, Mount Vesuvius, while Sicily's towering Mount Etna erupts with glowing lava rivers.
+++
In the north, pale Dolomite peaks glow soft pink every evening at sunset.
---
Italy — Rome. Mineral-rich volcanic soils around Vesuvius and Etna support lush terraced groves.

#6 [FR]
The European Union's tallest summit — snowy Mont Blanc at 4,808 meters — rises on this country's Alpine border, alongside the turquoise Verdon Gorge.
---
France — Paris. Along the Atlantic shore stands the Dune of Pilat, Europe's tallest shifting sand dune.

#7 [FI]
Over 180,000 sparkling lakes, quiet pine taiga forests, and Arctic Lapland — where summer midnight sun shines for weeks — define this northern nation.
---
Finland — Helsinki. Sprawling Lake Saimaa is home to one of the world's rarest freshwater seals.

#8 [SE]
More than 260,000 islands dot the coastlines of this Scandinavian country, while remote Sarek National Park shelters nearly a hundred wild glaciers.
---
Sweden — Stockholm. Its northern wilderness stretches deep beyond the Arctic Circle.

#9 [HR]
Hundreds of cascading waterfalls link sixteen terraced turquoise lakes inside Plitvice Lakes National Park in this Adriatic state.
+++
Over 1,200 rocky islands scatter across its clear sapphire coastline.
---
Croatia — Zagreb. Travertine limestone barriers continuously reshape the lakes and falls over centuries.

#10 [SI]
Glacial Lake Bled with its tiny church islet, the emerald Soča River, and three-peaked Mount Triglav shine in this compact Alpine nation.
---
Slovenia — Ljubljana. Dramatic underground canyons carve through UNESCO-listed Škocjan Caves.

#11 [AT]
This country holds the world's largest ice cave system, Eisriesenwelt, stretching over 40 kilometers, plus Central Europe's tallest waterfall at Krimml.
---
Austria — Vienna. The scenic Grossglockner High Alpine Road winds past more than thirty peaks over 3,000 meters.

#12 [GR]
Cloud-wrapped Mount Olympus rises in the north of this sunny land, while sheer stone pillars at Meteora tower abruptly above plains.
---
Greece — Athens. On Crete, the rugged Samaria Gorge winds 16 kilometers down to the Libyan Sea.

#13 [ES]
Snow-capped Pyrenees and Sierra Nevada peaks here contrast with lush Atlantic cliffs and mainland Europe's only true desert at Tabernas.
---
Spain — Madrid. Jagged limestone spires in the Picos de Europa rise just kilometers from the ocean.

#14 [PT]
Mainland Europe's westernmost point — windswept Cape Roca — stands 140 meters above crashing Atlantic waves right here.
---
Portugal — Lisbon. Far out in the ocean, its Azores islands hold twin green and blue crater lakes.

#15 [IE]
The 200-meter Cliffs of Moher plunge vertically into the Atlantic here, while lush fern-filled valleys thrive under mild Gulf Stream breezes.
---
Ireland — Dublin. Rugged Connemara peat bogs and Killarney oak woods harbor wild red deer.

#16 [GB]
Soaring white chalk cliffs at Dover, mysterious Scottish lochs, and heather-blanketed granite moorlands shape this island nation.
---
United Kingdom — London. Ben Nevis in the Scottish Highlands is the highest summit in the British Isles.

#17 [PL]
Shifting coastal sand dunes along the Baltic meet primeval Białowieża bison forests in this Central European nation.
+++
Sharp granite peaks in the High Tatras cradle crystal-clear alpine Lake Morskie Oko.
---
Poland — Warsaw. The Vistula Spit and Masurian Lake District attract birdwatchers from across Europe.

#18 [SK]
Sparkling underground galleries in Dobšinská Ice Cave and jagged High Tatra ridges make this mountainous state an explorer's paradise.
---
Slovakia — Bratislava. Slovak Paradise National Park features canyon trails climbed via metal ladders beside waterfalls.

#19 [RO]
A wide Carpathian mountain arc sweeps across this nation toward the Danube Delta — the European Union's vast wetland haven for pelicans.
---
Romania — Bucharest. The dramatic Transfăgărășan mountain pass zigzags past glacial lakes and alpine ridges.

#20 [ME]
This small Balkan state claims Europe's deepest canyon — the Tara River Gorge, plunging up to 1,300 meters below forested rims.
---
Montenegro — Podgorica. Rugged Durmitor massif hides 18 shimmering glacial lakes nicknamed "mountain eyes".

#21 [BG]
High in the Rila Mountains here sit seven terraced glacial lakes stepping down granite bowls over 2,000 meters above sea level.
---
Bulgaria — Sofia. Nearby Mount Musala rises to 2,925 meters as the highest peak of the entire Balkan Peninsula.

#22 [LV]
Europe's widest natural waterfall, Ventas Rumba — spanning over 240 meters across — flows gently over Devonian rock steps here.
---
Latvia — Riga. Gauja National Park is famed for warm red sandstone cliffs along winding forested rivers.

#23 [LT]
The slender Curonian Spit peninsula stretches nearly 100 kilometers of shifting white dunes and pine woods separating a quiet lagoon from the Baltic.
---
Lithuania — Vilnius. Ancient Čepkeliai marshlands shelter rare cranes, black storks, and wild orchids.

#24 [BY]
Nicknamed the "blue-eyed land" for its 10,000+ glacial lakes, this country guards Yelnya — a pristine raised peat bog older than Egypt's pyramids.
---
Belarus — Minsk. Vast flooded meadows along the Pripyat River serve as a major stopover for migrating birds.
