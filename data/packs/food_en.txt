#1 [UA]
The signature beet-and-cabbage soup of this country is so iconic that its cooking tradition was added to UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list.
+++
It is traditionally served warm alongside garlic buns called pampushky.
---
Ukraine — Kyiv. Borscht and filled dumplings called varenyky are beloved symbols of Ukrainian cuisine.

#2 [IT]
This country invented over 300 shapes of pasta, and its classic red, white, and green dish of tomato, mozzarella, and basil mirrors its national flag.
+++
Tiramisu and espresso coffee also come from here.
---
Italy — Rome. Traditional Neapolitan pizza-making is recognized by UNESCO worldwide.

#3 [FR]
Flaky crescent-shaped croissants anchor morning breakfasts in this country's capital, while its crusty baguette even has legal baking rules.
---
France — Paris. Its pastry shops are also famous for crème brûlée, colorful macarons, and eclairs.

#4 [ES]
Saffron-tinted rice cooked with vegetables or seafood in a wide shallow pan — paella — hails from the sunny Mediterranean coast of this nation.
+++
In hot summers, locals cool down with chilled tomato soup called gazpacho.
---
Spain — Madrid. Sweet fried dough sticks dipped in thick hot chocolate, known as churros, are another favorite.

#5 [PT]
Golden puff-pastry custard tarts sprinkled with cinnamon — pastéis de nata — were created centuries ago by monks in this Atlantic country.
---
Portugal — Lisbon. The famous historic bakery in Lisbon's Belém district has baked them since 1837.

#6 [BE]
Walk down any square in this small kingdom and you smell warm grid-patterned waffles alongside handmade pralines from world-famous chocolatiers.
---
Belgium — Brussels. Liège waffles caramelize outside thanks to crunchy pearl sugar baked inside the dough.

#7 [CH]
Friends in this Alpine country gather around a bubbling pot of melted cheese — fondue — dipping cubes of crusty bread on long forks.
---
Switzerland — Bern. It is equally renowned for smooth alpine milk chocolate and crispy potato rösti.

#8 [AT]
This country's capital invented both the rich apricot-layered Sacher chocolate cake and paper-thin golden breaded schnitzel.
---
Austria — Vienna. Warm cinnamon apple strudel is a classic companion in historic Viennese cafés.

#9 [DE]
Over 3,000 official bread varieties are baked across this country, while twisted salted pretzels shine in almost every bakery window.
---
Germany — Berlin. Its southwest forests also inspired the famous layered Black Forest cherry cake.

#10 [GR]
Briny Kalamata olives, crumbly white feta cheese, and honey-soaked nut pastry baklava define the sunny table of this Mediterranean country.
---
Greece — Athens. Classic village salad here tops juicy tomatoes and cucumbers with a whole slab of feta and olive oil.

#11 [PL]
Ring-shaped boiled-and-baked obwarzanek bread rolls scent the streets of Kraków, while families gather to fold stuffed dumplings called pierogi.
---
Poland — Warsaw. Jam-filled doughnuts called pączki spark huge lines outside bakeries on Fat Thursday.

#12 [HU]
Vibrant sweet red paprika powder gives unmistakable color and warmth to hearty goulash soup in this Central European nation.
+++
Grand cafés in its capital also serve multi-layered Dobos torte topped with hard caramel.
---
Hungary — Budapest. The southern city of Szeged even hosts an entire museum dedicated to paprika.

#13 [CZ]
Cinnamon and vanilla sugar fill the air around spiral chimney pastries called trdelník, while traditional meals feature fluffy bread dumplings.
---
Czechia — Prague. Sweet fruit-filled dumplings topped with melted butter and cottage cheese are a childhood classic here.

#14 [GB]
An iconic afternoon ritual on this island brings black tea paired with warm scones, clotted cream, and strawberry jam.
+++
Seaside towns also famously wrap crispy battered fish and thick chips in paper.
---
United Kingdom — London. Afternoon tea became a beloved social tradition here in the 1840s.

#15 [IE]
Bakers on this emerald island craft hearty soda bread without yeast, and Halloween tables feature fruit-packed barmbrack cake with a hidden ring inside.
---
Ireland — Dublin. Rich golden butter made from grass-fed pastures is exported around the globe.

#16 [NL]
Thin twin waffle wafers glued together with warm gooey caramel syrup — stroopwafels — were invented in the town of Gouda here.
---
Netherlands — Amsterdam. Gouda and Edam also lent their names to two of the world's most recognizable round cheeses.

#17 [DK]
Lunchtime art in this northern kingdom revolves around smørrebrød — colorful open-faced sandwiches piled neatly onto dense dark rye bread.
---
Denmark — Copenhagen. Flaky laminated sweet pastries are even known worldwide as "Danishes".

#18 [SE]
Daily life here treasures "fika" — a cozy unhurried pause for hot coffee or cocoa alongside a spiral cardamom or cinnamon bun.
---
Sweden — Stockholm. Families also love savory meatballs served with tart red lingonberry jam.

#19 [NO]
From this land of fjords comes brunost — an unusual sweet caramel-tasting brown whey cheese sliced into ribbons for toast and waffles.
---
Norway — Oslo. Cold Arctic waters off its coast also supply salmon prized by chefs everywhere.

#20 [LT]
A true showstopper at celebrations in this Baltic state is šakotis — a tall spiky tree-shaped cake baked slowly on a spinning spit over fire.
---
Lithuania — Vilnius. Bright pink chilled beet soup called šaltibarščiai is another local summer favorite.

#21 [BG]
Named for the unique beneficial culture behind its thick tangy yogurt, this Balkan country also bakes flaky cheese-filled banitsa spirals for breakfast.
---
Bulgaria — Sofia. Fresh Shopska salad crowned with grated white sirene cheese is a national staple.

#22 [RO]
Golden cornmeal polenta called mămăligă and tender cabbage rolls sarmale anchor warm family feasts across this Carpathian country.
---
Romania — Bucharest. Braided sweet bread cozonac swirled with walnuts and cocoa fills kitchens at holidays.

#23 [BA]
In the historic bazaar of Sarajevo, sizzling ćevapčići tucked into pillowy somun flatbread and flaky spiral burek draw long lines.
---
Bosnia and Herzegovina — Sarajevo. Strong coffee brewed slowly in copper džezva pots with Turkish delight ends every meal.

#24 [BY]
The humble potato takes center stage in this country's kitchen, transformed into crispy golden pancakes called draniki and hearty baked babka.
---
Belarus — Minsk. Thick cool sour cream is the classic partner on every plate of fresh draniki.
